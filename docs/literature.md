# Literature

## Slow-moving capital and gradual price discovery

| Paper | Finding | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Duffie (2010), *Journal of Finance*, "Asset price dynamics with slow-moving capital" | When capital cannot move instantly, prices adjust to shocks over days or weeks. | The core mechanism |
| Mitchell, Pedersen and Pulvino (2007), *AER P&P*, "Slow moving capital" | Specialist markets recover slowly from shocks because capital arrives slowly. | Munis are a specialist, retail-held market |
| Hong and Stein (1999), *Journal of Finance*, gradual information diffusion | News diffuses slowly across investor groups, causing under-reaction and momentum. | Catch-up and flow persistence |
| Dimson (1979), *Journal of Financial Economics*, "Risk measurement when shares are subject to infrequent trading" | Betas of thinly traded assets need lagged market returns. | The distributed-lag regression |

## Muni pricing

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| Choi, Kronlund and Oh (2022), *Journal of Financial Economics*, "Sitting bucks: Stale pricing in fixed income funds" | Bond fund NAVs, muni funds included, are stale because they use evaluated prices, and investors can exploit it through fund flows. This project finds the same slowness in exchange-traded muni ETF prices relative to Treasuries, tradeable in the open market. |
| MSRB, "Price Evaluations" | How vendor evaluated pricing for munis works and why it lags trading. |
| Muni ETF price and NAV gaps in March 2020 (Bond Buyer coverage) | Under stress, muni ETF prices and NAVs diverged sharply; the ETF moved first and NAVs caught up. |
| Muni market "reinvestment season" | Coupon and redemption cash concentrates at the start of months. The strategy uses only the start-of-month calendar. |
